Decisions this report supports

  • Detect new publications matching a saved research query
  • Review journal and publication-type coverage in a literature update
  • Export citation metadata into a human-reviewed screening workflow

Data limitation: PubMed citation metadata can be updated and does not reproduce article full text, establish evidence quality, or provide medical conclusions.
